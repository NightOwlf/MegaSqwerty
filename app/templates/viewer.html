    Display options

    Using the log viewer

    Click or drag on a graph
    Move the cursor. The values at the left edge follow it, with the highest (▲) and lowest (▼) beside each. On a phone, drag sideways to move through the log and up or down to scroll the page.
    Scroll wheel / pinch
    Zoom in and out around the pointer. Shift + wheel, or a sideways swipe, pans.
    On a phone
    Two fingers anywhere on a graph zoom and pan, one finger sideways moves through the log, up or down scrolls the page, double-tap zooms in. Tables fit the screen so you can watch the cells while you scrub; pinch a table to zoom into it (Fit table shows all of it again).
    Drag the strip under the graphs
    Move through the log. Drag across empty space to jump.
    Tap a channel name
    The coloured names at the top right of each graph: swap it for any other channel (the ones that matter for tuning are listed first), or remove it. Tap + to add one; up to four per graph. Layout sets up all the graphs at once for fuel, AFR, boost, ignition or warm-up.
    ← →
    Step one record. Hold Shift for ten.
    ↑ ↓
    Zoom in and out. F shows the whole log.
    Space
    Play and pause. Home and End jump to the ends.
    VE Analyze
    Plays through the log, works out what each VE cell should be from the wideband, and shows the cells changing as it goes. Then save the result as a new tune.
    Edit a table
    Tap or drag across cells, then type a value or use the buttons (+, −, ±1%, interpolate, smooth). With Auto-save on, every change goes into your own edited copy of the tune and the page's link switches to it.
    Remote tuning
    Edit, then Share: whoever opens your link sees the log with your edits, the changed cells marked in orange. If they edit it, they get their own copy to send back. The original tune is never changed.
    ✂ Section
    Choose parts of the log to tune: drag across a graph, or mark Start and End at the cursor. VE Analyze then uses only those, and the link keeps them.
    Cells show
    Turn a table into a map of the log: time in each cell, the most knock retard, or the average AFR error.
    🔒 Lock
    Select cells and lock them: VE Analyze won't change them. Filters & settings also has RPM, load and throttle windows, your own skip-when filters on any channel, and the strength of the correction.
    ● Live view
    Everyone on this log link sees the same moment: when anyone plays, pauses, scrubs or zooms, the others follow. Tap it for Private to go through the log on your own (the others don't follow you, and you don't follow them).
    Shape (3D)
    With a table in 3D, tap Shape, then drag a point up or down to raise or lower it; its neighbours follow a little, for a smooth change. Tap cells first (or Range) to move a whole area together, e.g. to change a slope. Tap Shape again to go back to turning the map.
    Compare
    Before, after and the % change of every table you or VE Analyze changed, side by side as tables or 3D, with a list of every changed cell.
    3D
    Shows a table as a surface. Drag to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-click to reset, tap a point to select that cell.
    ⚙ Display options
    The highest and lowest on screen or in the whole log, the graph scale, line and text size, and peak markers.
    Copy link
    The link opens at this exact moment with the same channels and zoom, so a friend sees what you see.
