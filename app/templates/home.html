What is this?

MSQ Viewer opens the .msq tune files that TunerStudio saves for MegaSquirt, Speeduino, rusEFI and FOME ECUs. You get VE, spark and AFR/lambda tables as heatmaps, every other setting in a searchable list, and a link you can post in a forum or Discord.

  • No account, nothing to install
  • Tap any cell for its RPM/load and value
  • Compare two tunes cell by cell
  • You get a delete key after uploading. Tunes nobody views for 180 days are removed.
